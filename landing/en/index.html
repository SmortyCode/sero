How it works

Three steps.
The last one is yours.

  1. 1

    Scan

    One photo is enough, more is better. SERO reads set, number, edition and language — and on slabs, the grader, grade and cert number straight from the label, whether it's PSA, CGC or BGS.

  2. 2

    Check

    A title buyers actually search for, category, price suggestion, shipping, cleanly cut-out photos — everything is waiting as a draft. Anything that's off, you tap and change.

  3. 3

    List

    One tap and the draft goes to your eBay account. It only goes live after your approval — never on its own, never unasked.

Home screen

Like an app,
without the App Store.

SERO runs in the browser. From your home screen it launches like an installed app — full screen, with its own icon.

iPhone Safari

  1. Open app.seromunich.com/app in Safari
  2. Tap the share icon at the bottom — the square with the arrow pointing up
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen” from the list
  4. Tap “Add” in the top right

Android Chrome

  1. Open app.seromunich.com/app in Chrome
  2. Tap the three-dot menu in the top right
  3. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”
  4. Confirm with “Install”

FAQ

Short answers.

Do I need an eBay account?

Yes, your own. SERO lists through your account — you connect it once, and SERO sets up your selling policies and shipping location along the way.

Does SERO publish on its own?

No. Every draft waits for your approval. Only your tap makes it live on eBay — there is no autopilot.

Where does the market value come from?

From real evidence: sold items and live listings matching edition, language and condition. When the evidence is thin, SERO shows “value unknown” and you set the price yourself. Honest beats invented.

Does it work with graded cards?

Yes. SERO reads grader, grade and cert number straight from the slab's label — PSA, CGC, BGS, WATA and more. The grade is part of the identity: what gets valued is the graded item, not the raw card.

Which categories does SERO cover?

SERO is most at home with trading cards, retro games, manga and comics. It also recognizes other items — electronics, toys, accessories — those drafts just deserve a closer look before you approve.

When is SERO coming to the App Store?

Soon. Until then, SERO runs as a web app in your browser and from your home screen — full feature set included. The guide above shows the way.
