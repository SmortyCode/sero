FAQ

Straight answers

Does SERO list on eBay by itself?

No. SERO builds a draft. You review the title, photos, and price, then publish when you are ready. Nothing goes live until you say so.

What can I photograph?

Trading cards, graded slabs, retro games, manga, and comics. eBay.de first. Other categories follow as they prove out.

Where do prices come from?

From real marketplace listings, not a language model. If there are not enough comps, SERO says value unknown instead of inventing a number.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Open SERO in the browser. On iPhone you can add it to the Home Screen later. App Store and Google Play are coming.

Is this only for Germany?

eBay.de and Euro first. The product is built in Munich. Other marketplaces are next, not live yet.

Add to Home Screen later

The app runs in the browser today. Native stores are coming.

  1. iPhone: Share in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: browser menu, then Add to Home screen.
